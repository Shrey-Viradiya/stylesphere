# Seasonal Color Palettes 2026: Fresh Hues to Try

2026 brings a balanced mixture of earthy neutrals, soft pastels, and pops of vibrant color. The key to adopting seasonal palettes is subtlety—use bright hues as accents and anchor looks with neutral tones.

## Palette Picks
- Earth & Clay: warm, grounding tones ideal for structured tailoring
- Soft Pastels: gentle lavenders and powder blues for relaxed daywear
- Vivid Accents: saturated oranges and teals for statement elements

## Styling Tips
Integrate new colors with small accessories or single garments before committing to full wardrobe changes. Color-blocking in small doses feels modern and confident.
