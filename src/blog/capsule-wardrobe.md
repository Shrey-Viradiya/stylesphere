# How to Build a Capsule Wardrobe: Timeless Pieces for Every Season

A capsule wardrobe focuses on quality, versatility, and timeless design. The goal is to reduce decision fatigue while elevating day-to-day style with fewer, better pieces.

## Key Pieces to Start With
- A tailored blazer in a neutral tone
- A pair of well-fitted jeans
- A classic white shirt
- A versatile dress or jumpsuit
- Neutral footwear that transitions from day to night

## Putting Outfits Together
Choose a color palette and stick to a mix of textures. Accessories like scarves and belts can transform looks while conserving closet space.

## Maintenance & Mindful Shopping
Invest in repairs, learn basic alterations, and replace only when necessary. Aim for pieces that become better with time.
